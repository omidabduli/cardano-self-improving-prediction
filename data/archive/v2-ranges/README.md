# Second record: 1, 3 and 24-hour forecasts with ranges

The live record from 25 to 26 September 2026, when ADAptive published a price estimate and an
80% range for 1 hour, 3 hours and 24 hours every 15 minutes, with the direction taken from the
ensemble. It is kept exactly as the pipeline committed it, together with the 30-day launch
backtest of that version (`backtest.json`). The current record (with the direction model) starts
fresh in `data/`.
