Method

How it predicts, and how it learns

In plain words. Nothing runs on a server: your browser does all of this with the published model. Every 15 minutes a GitHub job replays the same minutes with the same code and saves the official record.

  1. 01

    Look at the market

    It takes the last three days of minute-by-minute prices for ADA, Bitcoin and Ethereum, plus the daily crypto Fear & Greed index.

  2. 02

    Turn that into signals

    About 40 of them, like "ADA rose 2% in the last 6 hours", "Bitcoin just moved but ADA hasn't followed yet" or "the price is near the top of today's range".

  3. 03

    Ask six experts

    Each expert reads different signals and guesses the move. One of them, the Skeptic, always says "no change". Their weighted average is the price estimate.

  4. 04

    Draw the range

    How far ADA has been moving lately sets the width of the range. If it has swung about 1.5% an hour recently, the 1-hour range is about that wide.

  5. 05

    Check every forecast

    When a forecast's time comes, it is compared with the real price. Experts that were closer get more say next time, like listening more to the advisers who were right lately.

  6. 06

    Correct itself

    A range that missed gets wider and one that held gets narrower, until it holds 8 times out of 10. If it said "55% up" but such calls came true only half the time, it tones itself down.

  7. 07

    Retrain every night

    At midnight UTC every expert is retrained on the latest 60 days. Changed settings then compete with the current ones on the last ten days, and win only if they are clearly and consistently better.

An honest limit

Can it get close to 100%? No.

Nobody can reliably call the direction of a liquid market hours ahead. In a 300-day test, every combination of signals we tried called the direction between 48% and 54% of the time: noise around a coin flip. What can be forecast honestly is how far the price is likely to move. That is what the range shows, and it is checked every day.

If a real edge appears, the ensemble and the daily evolution are built to find it, and the record above will show it. This is a public experiment, not financial advice.
