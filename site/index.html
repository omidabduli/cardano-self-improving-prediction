Method

How it keeps correcting itself

Nothing runs on a server. Your browser runs the published model on the live Binance feed. A GitHub job, a few times a day, replays the same minutes with the same code and commits the official record.

  1. 01

    Read the market

    Minute candles for ADA, Bitcoin and Ethereum, plus the daily crypto Fear & Greed index. The signals cover the last 15 minutes to 3 days.

  2. 02

    Forecast

    At :00, :15, :30 and :45 six experts give their view. Their weighted vote becomes the price estimate. The range comes from how much ADA has been moving lately.

  3. 03

    Learn from every result

    When a forecast's time comes, it is scored. Experts that were closer gain trust. A range that missed widens, one that held narrows, and the stated probabilities are recalibrated.

  4. 04

    Evolve once a day

    At 00:00 UTC mutated settings challenge the current ones on the last ten unseen days. A challenger wins only with a clear, consistent lead. Then every expert is retrained.

An honest limit

Can it get close to 100%? No.

Nobody can reliably call the direction of a liquid market hours ahead. In a 300-day test, every combination of signals we tried called the direction between 48% and 54% of the time: noise around a coin flip. What can be forecast honestly is how far the price is likely to move. That is what the range shows, and it is checked every day.

If a real edge appears, the ensemble and the daily evolution are built to find it, and the record above will show it. This is a public experiment, not financial advice.
