Method

How it predicts, and how it learns

No server: your browser runs the published model on the live market. Every 15 minutes a GitHub job does the same and saves the official record.

  1. 01

    Look at the market

    The last week of minute-by-minute prices for Cardano, Bitcoin and Ethereum, plus the daily crypto Fear & Greed index.

  2. 02

    Call the direction

    A model that only learns whether the price went up or down, not by how much, so a few huge swings can't drown out the pattern.

  3. 03

    Put a number on it

    The call and how far ADA has been moving lately give one predicted price for 1 hour, 3 hours and 24 hours.

  4. 04

    Check every prediction

    When its time comes, each prediction is compared with the real price and scored in public.

  5. 05

    Learn every night

    At midnight UTC every model is retrained on the latest data. New settings only replace the old ones if they are clearly better on days they haven't seen.
