Method

How it works

The model runs in your browser. A GitHub job runs it too, every 15 minutes, and saves the record.

  1. 01

    Reads the market

    A week of minute prices for Cardano, Bitcoin and Ethereum, plus the Fear & Greed index.

  2. 02

    Guesses up or down

    It only learns the direction, not how far the price moves.

  3. 03

    Says how sure it is

    55% should be right about 55% of the time. It can't predict the size of a move, so the price estimate is just today's price. At 3 and 24 hours it was hardly better than a coin flip in testing, so take those lightly.

  4. 04

    Gets checked

    Every forecast is compared with the real price later. Forecasts made late after an outage don't count.

  5. 05

    Retrains every night

    New settings are only kept if they do better on days the model hasn't seen.
